## Project 01 — The Monolith & Flute Residence

* **Proposed Title:** The Monolith & Flute Residence
* **Client / Source Reference:** Mr. Aditya (`public/projects/Mr. Aditya/`)
* **Project Type:** Luxury Residential Interior / Contemporary Apartment
* **Architectural Style:** Contemporary Minimalist / Warm Urban Modernism

### Editorial Description
Designed with an emphasis on geometric clarity and understated luxury, this residence balances monolithic surfaces with refined linear textures. The interior composition juxtaposes bookmatched marble-patterned finishes against vertical fluted timber, dark powder-coated steel frameworks, and tactile fluted glass partitions. Concealed architectural cove lighting, integrated niche daybeds, and disciplined neutral palettes punctuated by deep teal accents create a serene, light-filled urban sanctuary.

### Key Visual Features
* **Integrated Daybed Joinery:** Full-height dual-tone wardrobe wall incorporating a cozy upholstered bay seating and reading nook.
* **Architectural Steel & Reeded Partition:** Floor-to-ceiling spatial divider constructed from black steel box sections, reeded/fluted glass, and marble-clad panels.
* **Monolithic Media Backdrop:** Bookmatched white marble TV feature wall accented with dark vertical reveal grooves, low-profile floating media credenza, and open display grid shelving.
* **Bespoke Glass Mandir Unit:** Arched fluted-glass prayer cabinet with circular brass ring pulls and warm interior glow.
* **Curated Study & Vanity Suite:** Secondary bedroom workstation featuring high-gloss pearl cabinetry, custom semicircular teal pulls, an elongated capsule mirror, and floating geometric wall niches.

## Project 02 — The Sacred & Contemporary Abode

* **Proposed Title:** The Sacred & Contemporary Abode
* **Client / Source Reference:** Mr.Mansa (`public/projects/Mr.Mansa/`)
* **Project Type:** Luxury Residential Interior / Contemporary Indian Home
* **Architectural Style:** Transitional Indian Modernism / Contemporary Heritage Fusion

### Editorial Description
A sophisticated dialogue between classical Indian sensibilities and contemporary architectural joinery defines this spacious family residence. Rich natural teakwood accents, sculpted wooden diwan seating, and consecrated marble prayer sanctuaries coexist effortlessly with minimalist metal partition screens and curved marble paneling. Warm perimeter cove lighting, slatted timber-and-mirror ceiling treatments, and thoughtful color zoning—from powder blue in the culinary zone to dusty rose in the children's suite—deliver a deeply rooted yet modern living experience.

### Key Visual Features
* **Monumental Prayer Sanctum:** Central fluted marble backdrop with perimeter halo backlighting, laser-cut lotus-Om emblem, suspended solid brass bells, hand-carved wooden jali screen, and rich walnut base credenza.
* **Curved Marble Feature Wall:** Statuario marble TV backdrop with soft-radiused edges, acoustic fluted timber battens, and an integrated glass vitrine.
* **Layered Threshold Partition:** Multi-functional spatial divider integrating slatted timber ceilings, inset mirrored panels, open steel cubic frames, and a reeded-glass vitrine with an illuminated marble display pedestal.
* **Themed Children's Suite:** Whimsical twin bedroom featuring arched wainscoting, landscape mural wallpaper, dusty-rose study cabinetry, and a geometric diamond-shaped cove ceiling.
* **Contemporary Ergonomic Kitchen:** Handleless modular kitchen in powder blue with jet black granite countertops, dark textured stone splashback, and overhead display shelving.

## Project 03 — The Tridasa Arches

* **Proposed Title:** The Tridasa Arches
* **Client / Source Reference:** my home tridaas (`public/projects/my home tridaas/`)
* **Project Type:** High-End High-Rise Residential Interior
* **Architectural Style:** Neo-Classical Modernism / Warm Sculptural Contemporary

### Editorial Description
Embodying architectural poise and spatial continuity, this high-rise residence celebrates classical arched motifs reinterpreted through contemporary joinery and refined lighting. A rhythmic sequence of architectural Roman arches delineates formal dining from private living areas, complementing textured lime-wash feature walls and custom slatted timber ceilings. From the radiant concentric geometry of the marble mandir to the fluted wood and curved transitions of the master suite, the home balances meditative tranquility with functional elegance.

### Key Visual Features
* **Triple Roman Arch Colonnade:** Symmetrical architectural screen comprising one timber-lined passage portal and two recessed display arches with floating oak shelves and lower credenza storage.
* **Radiant Concentric Mandir:** Consecrated prayer niche featuring concentric stepped circular reveals in white marble, halo cove lighting, golden Om emblem, and hanging brass temple bells.
* **Sculptural Foyer Console:** Entry gallery featuring a fluted white marble column, brass frame, meditative white Buddha sculpture, and suspended globe luminaires against a lime-plaster wall.
* **Master Suite Retreat:** Low-profile upholstered platform bed, vertical fluted teak wall cladding merging into a curved headboard panel with dark reveal grooves, and a floating study station.
* **Timber & Mirror Ceiling Grid:** Overhead slatted timber treatment with mirrored panels expanding vertical volume and introducing warm rhythm across the central axis.
